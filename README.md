## Kalsooni

I founded Kalsooni ICT Solutions to build **Kalsooni**, a multi-tenant ERP sold as a subscription to businesses in Somalia. It covers accounting, stock, selling, buying, point of sale, HR and payroll, and fixed assets. It takes mobile-money payments through EVC Plus, ZAAD, SAHAL and eDahab, works in USD, SOS and SLSH, and is available in English and Somali.

It's designed and documented, with more than 100 recorded design decisions, a full brand kit and draft legal documents, and I'm building it now. The first pilot customers are planned for February 2027.

Planned stack: Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL with one database per tenant, Prisma, Better Auth, Redis, BullMQ, Bun and Docker, on Hetzner servers managed with OpenTofu, cloud-init and Ansible. An Expo mobile app comes later.
